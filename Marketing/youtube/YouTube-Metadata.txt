Title (100 max)
    openWater: a free, open-source session tracker for wingfoil, windsurf, kite, SUP and sailing

Description
    openWater records your session on iPhone or Apple Watch, draws what actually
    happened on the map, and measures every speed category riders compare — 2 s,
    10 s, 500 m, the nautical mile, Alpha 500 — plus foiling, gybes, upwind angles,
    glides and jumps. Spots is a guide to 1000+ launches with live wind on every pin,
    a wind-wash forecast you can turn three days ahead, tides, currents, swell,
    radar and beach cameras. The same picture fills the big screen on Apple TV.

    No account. No subscription. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.
    Open source.

    App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/openwater
    Website:   https://openwaterapp.com
    Spot guide: https://openwaterapp.com/spots
